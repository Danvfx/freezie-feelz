About us

Your Mood, Your Flavour, Your FreezieFeelz

FreezieFeelz is a student-led drink business from La Salle College Antipolo. We took the Yakult you already know and gave it a fruity, fizzy twist, so you can sip the flavour that matches how you feel.

Feeling a bit blue? Blueberry has you. Feeling sweet? Try Strawberry. Every drink is made with Yakult, a probiotic drink, plus Sprite, fruit flavouring and real fruit pieces, then served cold with ice.

Our colours mean something too. Blue stands for loyalty and trust. Icy blue stands for freshness and cool.

  • 7flavours to pick from
  • 100%student-made
  • ₱55starting price

Our mission

FreezieFeelz is dedicated to providing refreshing, affordable, and high-quality drinks that match every customer's mood and cravings. We aim to create enjoyable flavors using quality ingredients while giving customers great value for their money.

Our vision

FreezieFeelz aims to become a well-known and trusted beverage brand by offering fun, creative, and refreshing drinks that bring happiness to every customer. We envision expanding our reach from La Salle College of Antipolo, making every sip a delightful experience.
